Projects

A few things I have built while studying machine learning and systems.

EcoCAR · Road-object detection

Built a YAML-configurable, CLI-driven PyTorch and Ultralytics YOLO pipeline to audit, train, benchmark, select, and run detectors on 860 road images with 3,418 labeled objects across 14 classes.

  • Fine-tuned YOLO26n, YOLO26s, and YOLO26m under controlled settings and selected YOLO26s using mAP50–95, recall, latency, and model-size tie-breakers.
  • Evaluated the frozen winner on 86 untouched test images: 40.75% mAP50–95, 72.40% precision, 80.30% recall, and 76.15% F1.
  • Added 42 Pytest tests, dataset fingerprinting, and SHA-256 integrity checks.

PyTorch, Ultralytics YOLO, YAML, Pytest

LatentLink · Optical transfer

Built a VQ-VAE image-transfer pipeline that compresses 512 × 512 RGB images from 768 KB to about 4 KB while retaining 27.4 dB PSNR.

  • Transmits compressed images through about 27 QR frames.
  • Built a PyTorch and FastAPI inference service with QR-based transfer, CRC32 validation, sequence tracking, and retransmission for missing frames.

VQ-VAE, PyTorch, FastAPI, QR codes, CRC32

JobConnect · AI job matching

Led AI/ML development for a two-person team building an explainable job-matching assistant for the Mississippi Department of Employment Security.

  • Built resume-to-job matching around embedding search, requirement scoring, preference filters, confidence, and audit logging.
  • Designed evaluation workflows using de-identified resumes and human-reviewed resume-to-job matches.

Embedding search, hybrid ranking, explainable AI
